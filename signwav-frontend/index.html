About Us

SIGN-WAV is dedicated to breaking down communication barriers between the deaf community and the hearing world. Our innovative platform combines cutting-edge technology with user-friendly design to make sign language interpretation accessible to everyone.

We believe in creating a more inclusive world where everyone can communicate freely, regardless of their hearing ability. Our advanced gesture recognition system supports both static and dynamic sign language interpretation, making it easier than ever to bridge the communication gap.
